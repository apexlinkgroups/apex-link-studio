FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before placing an order.

What file formats do you accept?

We accept all major photo and video formats — JPG, PNG, TIFF, RAW (CR2, ARW, NEF), PSD for photos; MP4, MOV, MXF, ProRes, R3D for video. For large files or batches, simply share a Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer link in the order form.

How does delivery work?

Once your order is confirmed, we begin editing immediately. Standard delivery is 48-72 hours from receipt. Rush orders (24-hour) are available at an additional charge. You'll receive a download link via email when files are ready — in full resolution, export-ready for your platform.

How many revisions are included?

Starter plans include 1 revision round. Pro plans include 3 revisions. Enterprise and Long Form clients get unlimited revisions — we iterate until you're 100% satisfied. Additional revision rounds on Starter/Pro can be purchased à la carte.

Is my content kept confidential?

Absolutely. All files are transferred via 256-bit encrypted channels and stored on secured servers. We sign NDAs on request for commercial and brand clients. Your content is never shared, used in our portfolio, or shown publicly without your explicit written consent.

Do you offer ongoing / monthly plans?

Yes — our Enterprise tier is designed for recurring work. We assign a dedicated editor to your brand, set up a shared style guide, and invoice monthly. Volume discounts apply. Contact us to discuss a custom retainer that fits your workflow and budget.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept PayPal, Stripe (all major credit/debit cards), and bank transfer for Enterprise clients. Payment is required before delivery. A 50% deposit can be arranged for large projects over $500. All prices are in USD.
